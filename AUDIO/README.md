# AUDIO: voiceover-ready tracks (no narration)

| File | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `VOICEOVER_OWNER.wav` | **to be supplied by the owner.** Not created on purpose | Egyptian Arabic recording by the business owner. Drop it in this folder with exactly this name. The After Effects builder imports it onto the `VOICEOVER_OWNER` track automatically. If it is missing, an empty labelled placeholder track is created. |
| `BACKGROUND_MUSIC.wav` | ✅ | original instrumental score, no vocals, 48 kHz/16-bit stereo, 35.0 s. −20.5 LUFS, peak −6.4 dBFS. A 1–3 kHz scoop leaves room for the voice. |
| `SOUND_EFFECTS.wav` | ✅ | metro and city ambience, soft whooshes on the cuts, low gentle GPS chimes (880/1175 Hz), reveal swell and low impact (no high crack). −31 LUFS. |
| `MUSIC_SFX_REFERENCE_MIX.wav` | ✅ | music + SFX summed (−20.2 LUFS, peak −6 dBFS). This is the audio of the `MUSIC_SFX_noVO` export. |

Both stems share one gain, so in After Effects they play at 0 dB and sum exactly to the reference mix. Nothing is
synthesised as speech, and no AI voice was used. All music and SFX are generated by `tools/make_audio.py`, so there are no licensing issues.

## Recording guide for the owner

- Format: WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit (16-bit is fine), mono or stereo. Quiet room, phone or mic 15–20 cm away.
- Aim the delivery at about −16 LUFS (normal confident speaking voice). The bed is at −20 LUFS, so the voice will sit clearly on top.
- The suggested windows are where each headline is on screen. They match the scene markers in AE. The gaps between them are intentional pauses.

| Window (s) | On screen | Suggested idea (free to change, in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 – 5.0 | نازل محطة Cimiano M2؟ | the question to the viewer |
| 5.3 – 9.8 | تعالى نوصلك لمكتبنا | invitation |
| 10.5 – 16.5 | الطريق أسهل مما تتخيل! | the route is easy, follow toward Via Padova |
| 17.4 – 21.9 | وجهتك Puntocom | destination: Via Padova 167 |
| 24.5 – 28.6 | وصلت! إحنا في انتظارك | arrival plus services (CAF, Patronato, immigration, accounting) |
| 29.6 – 34.6 | PUNTOCOM · Via Padova 167 – Milano · مستنيينك! | brand sign-off |

The timeline is flexible. If the recording runs longer or shorter, slide the scene precomps in `00_MAIN_VO_READY_1080x1920`
(each scene is one layer), or keyframe the `MUSIC LEVEL dB` slider on the BACKGROUND_MUSIC layer to dip the music under the voice.

### Without After Effects (FFmpeg)
```bash
# voice + music + SFX onto the silent master (voice +0 dB, bed −3 dB under it)
ffmpeg -i 08_render/PUNTOCOM_AR_TheWayToUs_SILENT_1080x1920_30fps.mp4 -i AUDIO/VOICEOVER_OWNER.wav -i AUDIO/MUSIC_SFX_REFERENCE_MIX.wav \
  -filter_complex "[2:a]volume=-3dB[bed];[1:a][bed]amix=inputs=2:normalize=0:duration=longest,atrim=0:35[a]" \
  -map 0:v -map "[a]" -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 256k -shortest 08_render/PUNTOCOM_AR_WITH_OWNER_VO.mp4
```
