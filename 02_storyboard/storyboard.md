# Storyboard: PUNTOCOM – THE WAY TO US (35 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps)

Visual frames from the actual render are in `02_storyboard/storyboard_frames.jpg`, one per beat.

**Two identities.** The **journey** (S01–S05) uses Orange #F58220, Black #111111 and White, and every
wayfinding element is orange. The **brand** (S06) uses navy (sampled from the logo, #0C1620), the logo's
original gold and white. **The bridge (28.6–29.2 s):** the orange GPS line sweeps up the frame and shifts
from orange to gold as the navy world arrives. The route visually "becomes" the brand.

A thin orange progress rail at the top runs CIMIANO M2 → VIA PADOVA 167 from 0.4 s to 28.6 s, so the viewer
always knows where the journey is.

## Integrity rules applied
- Storefront: the photo is only scaled and translated. All graphics sit in the plate bands above and below the photo,
  or on the pavement. Nothing covers the signs, door or windows.
- Logo: the extracted original layers are only scaled, faded, blurred during the entrance, or wipe-masked.
  There is no recolouring or retyping.
- Text: Italian spelling checked (Raggiungerci è, CONTABILITÀ, Via Padova 167 – Milano). No Arabic text is
  added. The real Arabic signage is visible on the storefront photo, untouched.
