# Storyboard: PUNTOCOM – THE WAY TO US · Egyptian Arabic edition (35 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps)

Frames from the actual render are in `02_storyboard/storyboard_frames.jpg`.

**Voiceover-ready.** There is no narration. The headline windows below are also the suggested windows for the owner's voiceover
(they are comp markers in AE). The gaps between them (about 0.3–0.7 s, plus the longer 22–24.5 s arrival beat) are deliberate pauses.

**Reading direction.** Everything that moves follows Arabic reading order. Headlines wipe in from right to left, step cards
sit on the right and slide in from the right, and the journey progress rail runs from **Cimiano M2 (right)** to **Via Padova 167 (left)**.

**Two identities.** The journey (S1–S5) uses Orange #F58220 / Black #111111 / White. The brand (S6) uses navy (sampled from the logo), the original gold and white.
At 28.6–29.2 s the orange line sweeps up and turns gold as the navy world arrives.

## Integrity rules applied
- **Storefront:** the photo is only scaled and translated. All text sits in the plate bands above and below it. Signs, door and windows (including the real Arabic and Italian signage) are untouched.
- **Logo:** the original extracted layers are only scaled, faded or masked. Nothing is retyped or recoloured.
- **Arabic:** every string is shaped by HarfBuzz with FriBiDi bidi (Pillow + libraqm). Letters are joined, and mixed Arabic/Latin lines are ordered correctly.
- **Language:** no Italian narration or Italian promotional text remains.
